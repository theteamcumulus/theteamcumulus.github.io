# Lifestamp Support

## Frequently asked questions

**The camera screen says Lifestamp needs camera access.**
Turn on Camera in Settings > Lifestamp (iPhone) or Settings > Apps > Lifestamp > Permissions (Android), or tap Open Settings on the camera screen.

**My photos aren't in the Photos app (the gallery on Android).**
Check that auto-save is on in Settings (the gear at the top right of the camera screen). On iPhone, Settings > Lifestamp > Photos must also be set to "Add Photos Only". On Android, photos are saved to the Lifestamp album in your gallery (Pictures/Lifestamp) and no photo or storage permission is needed. You can also open a photo in the app's gallery and save it with the Share button.

**The weather doesn't show on my stamp.**
Weather appears only when location access is allowed and the app could fetch it over the internet. Check the location permission (iPhone: Settings > Lifestamp > Location; Android: Settings > Apps > Lifestamp > Permissions > Location, approximate location is enough) and "Show Weather on Stamp" in the settings at the top right of the camera screen. If the weather couldn't be fetched, the photo is saved without it and it can't be added later. Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com).

**Can I change the stamp style after taking a photo?**
Yes. Open the photo in the gallery and tap Change Stamp to pick another style or aspect ratio, or show or hide the time and weather. The original is kept, so you can change it as many times as you like.

**How do I change the aspect ratio?**
Tap the ratio button (9:16) at the top left of the camera screen to cycle through 9:16, 4:5, 1:1, and 3:4. The default 9:16 fits Instagram Stories exactly, and the stamp sits slightly inward so the story controls at the top and bottom don't cover it.

**Which time does the stamp use?**
The date, time and time zone of your device at the moment you press the shutter.

**What happens when I shoot in landscape?**
Turn your device (iPhone or Android) sideways and the stamp rotates to match the photo. The app's screen itself stays in portrait.

**Can I add a stamp to photos already in the Photos app?**
No. To record the exact moment of capture, Lifestamp only stamps photos taken in the app.

**I deleted a photo in the app, but it's still in Photos.**
On iPhone, Lifestamp can only add photos to the Photos app, so it can't delete the copy there. Please delete it in Photos. On Android, the copy saved to your gallery is not deleted by the app either, so delete it in the gallery.

**Will my photos be deleted if I delete the app?**
Photos in the app's gallery are deleted along with the app. Copies saved to the Photos app (the gallery on Android) stay.

**Can I move my photos to a new phone?**
On iPhone, the app's gallery (including the unstamped originals) is part of your iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), so it moves over when you set up a new iPhone from that backup. On Android, the app's storage is excluded from Google backup and device-to-device transfer. We can't guarantee how a phone maker's own migration tools behave. With auto-save on, stamped photos are also kept in the Photos app (the gallery on Android), and you can send them elsewhere with the Share button.

**Why are the stamps in English?**
The date format on each stamp is part of its design, inspired by film cameras and camcorders, so it looks the same in every language.
