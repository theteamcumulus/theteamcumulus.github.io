Kindly at a glance

Kindly is a free iPhone app with a daily quote and Bible verse widget and an anonymous community for sharing gratitude and affirmations.

Great for

  • People who want one quote or Bible verse a day on a Home Screen widget
  • Anyone who wants to share gratitude and affirmations anonymously
  • People who'd rather not sign up for an account

Not the right fit if

  • You want the app in a language other than Korean (the app and its quotes are in Korean)
  • You use an Android phone (there is no Android version)
  • Your iPhone runs a version earlier than iOS 17

FAQ

What is Kindly?

An iPhone app that shows one good quote or Bible verse a day in the app and on a Home Screen widget, together with an anonymous community for sharing gratitude and affirmations. Free, iOS 17 or later.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap + in the top-left corner and choose Kindly. Medium and large widgets have next, favourite and share buttons.

Do I need an account?

No. An anonymous per-device account is created on first launch. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.

I saw a post that bothers me.

Use the ⋯ menu on the post to report it or block the author. Blocked authors disappear right away; posts reported by several people are hidden and reviewed.

Which Bible translation is used?

Bible verses are quoted exactly from the Korean Revised Version (개역개정) published by the Korean Bible Society. Quotes are shown in Korean.

Why was my post saved as "Only me"?

Very short or unclear posts and negative posts are kept visible only to you. Add a sentence about what you are grateful for and why, and it can be shared. Repeating the same post and posting too often (3 per minute, 20 per day) are blocked.

Can I collect quotes and posts I like?

Tap the heart on a quote to browse your favourites as a topic in Today's Quote (the widget follows it too). Bookmark community posts to find them in Collection › Bookmarks.

How is my data handled?

Favourites and themes stay on your device. Community posts and reactions are stored on our server and you can delete everything in Settings › Delete account and data. We collect only anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, and never use the advertising identifier (IDFA). See the Privacy Policy.

Is Kindly available in English or on Android?

The app is in Korean only and runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. There is no Android version.
