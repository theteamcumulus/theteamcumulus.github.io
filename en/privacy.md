# Team Cumulus Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · [한국어](/privacy/)

Team Cumulus ("we") respects your privacy and follows the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy covers our website (cumulus.team) and all of our apps. ## 2. Summary of what our apps handle

- Our apps have no sign-up and do not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address or phone number.
- To keep the apps stable and improve them, we collect crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days and usage statistics after 2 months.
- We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not track you across other apps or websites.
- What you create in an app (alarms, photos, comfort records, sorting decisions and so on) stays on your device. Features that store data on a server, such as the Kindly community, list the items, retention period and how to delete them in that app's policy.

## 3. Website

- This website has no sign-up and uses no cookies or visitor analytics, and we do not collect information about visitors.
- The website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may process access logs such as visitors' IP addresses for security and operations, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 4. Email inquiries

- When you email us, we use your email address, the message and any app version or device details you include only to answer you and improve our apps.
- We keep inquiry emails only as long as needed to answer and follow up, and delete them without delay if you ask.
- Mail sent to cumulus.team addresses is forwarded to our mailbox through Cloudflare, Inc. Email Routing.

## 6. Deletion

When the retention period ends or you ask us to delete your information, we delete it without delay. Electronic files are erased so that they cannot be recovered.

## 7. Your rights

You may ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your information. Email us at the address below and we will handle your request without delay. Once you delete an app, it no longer collects any information.

## 8. Children

Our apps are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.
