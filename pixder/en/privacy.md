# Pixder Privacy Policy

Effective: October 12, 2026 (from version 1.0.0 on iPhone and Android) · [한국어](/pixder/privacy/)

Pixder ("the app") does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address or phone number. Your photos are read on your device only to show them and are never sent anywhere. To keep the app stable and improve it, we collect only the error reports and anonymous usage statistics described below.

## 1. Information we collect and why

| Type | What is collected | Purpose | Service |
|---|---|---|---|
| Error reports | Error logs when the app crashes or hits an error (error type and code), device model, OS version, app version | Finding and fixing bugs | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Usage statistics | Screen views and feature events (for example: opening a sort, totals such as how many photos you swiped, kept, favorited or marked for deletion, confirming or cancelling a deletion), a randomly generated app installation ID, device and OS information, approximate region estimated from your IP address (the IP address itself is not stored) | Understanding how features are used and improving the app | Google Analytics for Firebase |

We do not collect:

- Your photos and videos, their identifiers, capture dates, locations or any other information in them
- Which photos you kept or deleted
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android), contacts or precise location
- Your activity in other apps or on websites (we do not track you)

## 2. Information stored on your device

- Sorting records: whether you chose "keep", "favorite" or "delete" for each photo (the photo library item identifier and your choice), the last sort you were working on, and how many photos you've deleted. These are stored only in the app's storage and never sent anywhere.
- The app does not create copies or thumbnail files of your photos.
- If you use iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), your sorting records are included in the backup. Backups are managed by Apple and you; we cannot access them. On Android, sorting records are excluded from Google backup and device transfer.
- Deleting the app deletes your sorting records. Photos in your library are not affected.

## 3. Photo access and deletion

- The app asks for access to your photo library to show your photos one at a time and to delete the photos you choose. If you choose Limited Access, the app works only with the photos you selected.
- Photos are deleted only when you tap Delete on the review screen and confirm again in the system dialog.
  - iPhone: deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days and can be recovered during that time. If you use iCloud Photos, they're also deleted from your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
  - Android: deleted photos move to the trash for 30 days, after which the system removes them. Only photos on the device are deleted; cloud backups are managed in their own app.
- Photos you swipe up are added to Favorites. On Android, the system asks once when you leave the deck screen.

## 4. Retention

- Error reports: deleted automatically 90 days after collection
- Usage statistics: deleted automatically 2 months after collection

## 5. Processors and international transfer

This information is processed through Firebase, a service provided by Google LLC, and is transferred to and stored on Google servers in the United States and other countries.

- Recipient: Google LLC (privacy@google.com)
- Countries: the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers
- Data: the error reports and usage statistics in section 1
- When and how: from time to time while you use the app, over an encrypted network connection
- Purpose and retention: as in sections 1 and 4

For how Google handles this information, see https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

## 6. Feedback

Tapping Send Feedback in the settings menu opens your Mail app with the app version, device model and OS version filled in. No photo information is included, and nothing is sent unless you tap Send. We use feedback emails only to reply and improve the app.

## 7. Your rights

The information we collect does not directly identify you, but if you'd like it deleted or its processing stopped, email us at the address below. Once you delete the app, no further information is collected.

## 8. Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
