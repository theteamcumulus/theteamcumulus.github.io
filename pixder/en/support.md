# Pixder Support

Pixder is an iPhone photo cleaner: swipe photos one at a time — left to mark for deletion, right to keep. [About the app and full FAQ](/pixder/en/) · [한국어](/pixder/support/)

## FAQ

**I don't see any photos.**
Go to Settings > Pixder > Photos and make sure access isn't set to None. Choose Full Access or Limited Access, then return to the app.

**Only some of my photos show up.**
With Limited Access, Pixder can sort only the photos you selected. Tap Select More Photos on the home screen, or switch to Full Access in Settings.

**I swiped left but the photo is still in Photos.**
That's expected. Swiping only marks photos for deletion. To delete them, tap the trash button at the top right, then Delete, and confirm in the iOS dialog.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Open Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted and recover it within 30 days.

**I deleted photos but my storage didn't change.**
Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days and still use space. To free it now, delete all items in Recently Deleted in the Photos app.

**A photo looks blurry or says "Couldn't load preview".**
iCloud Photos that aren't stored on your iPhone are downloaded over the internet. Check your connection. You can still swipe and decide without a preview.

**I want to start over.**
When you finish a sort, tap Start Over on the completion screen to clear your choices for it. No photos are deleted.
